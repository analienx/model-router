# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Make it easy for people with any level of experience to take part.
- Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@analienx](https://github.com/analienx), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Don't report it in a public issue. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
